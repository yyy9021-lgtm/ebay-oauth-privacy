Privacy Policy

Melty Japan Inventory Sync is an internal inventory management application used by the application owner to manage their own eBay seller account.

Information Used

The application accesses eBay account, listing, and inventory information only as necessary to support inventory synchronization and listing management.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used only for inventory synchronization, listing management, and related operational purposes for the application owner's eBay seller account.

Authentication and Credentials

eBay credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and other authentication information are not sold or shared with third parties. Authentication credentials and tokens are stored securely and are used only for authorized access to eBay APIs.

Data Retention

Information obtained through eBay APIs is retained only as necessary for inventory and listing management and is not retained longer than reasonably needed for those purposes.

Access Revocation

Access granted to this application may be revoked through the applicable eBay account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
